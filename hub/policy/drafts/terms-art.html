6. Art on the hub

The hub shows ANSI art: its own, BBSes' (their splash screens, their small emblems for the map, and art they publish from their Bluesky accounts), and people's Bluesky profile pictures on the map. It also shows 3D models BBSes publish from their accounts, each with a picture that stands for it. It keeps copies of what it shows, checked and converted (the art cleaned of anything but characters and colours, and drawn as pictures; a model's pictures stripped of the details cameras and editors add, such as where a photo was taken), and serves them from its own address. In this section, "art" means all of these.

  • Only what's yours to share. Offer or publish only art you made, or have the right to share this way, under the licence you name for it. The credit and licence you give must be true. Art must keep to the rules above, including the hub's rating.
  • What you let the hub do. When your BBS offers the hub its splash screen or emblem (turned on when you connect its account, and off from its board account screen), or publishes art the hub takes, you let the hub copy, store, convert and show that art, with its credit and licence, on the hub's pages, its map and its chat (and, for a model, let people's browsers draw it, turn it and play its animations), and let people there view and save it as its licence allows, for as long as it's offered. This is not exclusive, costs nothing, and gives the hub no other rights to it.
  • Taking it back. Stop offering a piece (turn the splash screen off, or delete the record that publishes it) and the hub stops showing it within minutes and deletes its copies within the hour; copies in backups go as those backups do (see the privacy notice). Licences you gave others, such as Creative Commons, aren't undone by this.
  • Your picture. While you're shown in the common space as your own account, the map draws you with your Bluesky profile picture, unless you turn that off on your profile.
  • Takedowns. Report art that breaks these rules, or was copied without permission, with the Report link beside it or the report form; copyright notices go as section 8 says. The hub may stop showing any piece of art, refuse the same art again, or refuse all art from an account, with or without notice.
  • Uploads. The hub doesn't take art uploaded to it directly; art comes from BBSes and accounts, which keep the originals.
