1. What the hub is

The hub gives BBSes (bulletin board systems) an address under hub.cyberspace.nexus and carries visitors to them through a tunnel, so a BBS can run on a home computer without opening any ports. It also keeps a directory of BBSes, and a common space where people from those BBSes can see who's around and chat. You sign in with an AT Protocol account (such as a Bluesky account); the hub never sees your password.

The hub is free, run by one person, and offered as it is. It may change, pause, or stop.
